## Team Charter

From the initial team meeting, which can be reviewed in ["Appendix: Team Organization"](https://embedded-systems-design.github.io/EGR304TeamTemplate/Appendix/App-Team-Org/), the team's charter formed to be:

> "Our team measures our success based on the effort we put forth to achieve our goals and help our teammates. We strive to address every problem we encounter with integrity, accountability, and optimism so that we may learn, improve, and deliver a carefully desgined engineering project."

## Product Mission Statement

Our team discussed that the purpose of our product should be to automate a common household task, saving users time and allowing them to spend their energy elsewhere. We identified folding laundry as a repetitive and time-consuming task that could benefit from automation. From this discussion, we decided to develop a compact laundry folding robot intended for household users. The device will automatically fold common garments, reducing the time and effort associated with manual folding while providing consistent and neatly folded results. From these goals, we developed the following mission statement:

 > "Our mission is to develop an affordable and reliable automated laundry folding robot that reduces the time and effort required for household users to fold clothing, allowing them to dedicate more time to higher-value activities."
